# Nonpartisan race copy review

Reviewed October 11, 2026. The review establishes office-type wording; it does not refresh candidate records or confirm printed ballot names.

- Nebraska Secretary of State lists school boards, city councils, Legislature, State Board of Education, and public power districts among nonpartisan races: https://sos.nebraska.gov/elections/how-nonpartisan-voting-works-nebraska-primary-elections
- Nebraska Revised Statute 32-557 provides nonpartisan city, village, and school elections, with a city or village ordinance exception: https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=32-557
- Township boards are elected on nonpartisan ballots under 32-530: https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=32-530
- Educational service unit boards use nonpartisan ballots under 32-515: https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=32-515

The stored directory marks the named covered offices Nonpartisan. General county, state, and federal races also include partisan offices, so the introduction does not describe the whole directory as nonpartisan. It retains selected-race coverage, declared write-in inclusion, and location-dependent ballot scope.
