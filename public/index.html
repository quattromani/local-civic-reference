Local Civic Reference

Not every local race lists party affiliation on the ballot.

Some voters consider a candidate’s political affiliation along with their experience, qualifications, views, and plans. The people elected may make decisions about public services, spending, and taxes. This guide covers selected 2026 races in Gage County, including declared write-in candidates, and links to the sources used to check political affiliation. The races on your ballot depend on where you live.

About this guide

How to read this directory

Published by the Gage County GOP. The directory covers selected races, not every contest on your ballot.

Information, not endorsement

Candidates receive the same sourcing and presentation standards. Inclusion in this directory does not indicate a Gage County GOP endorsement.

How information is verified

Different sources establish different facts. Candidate cards identify the source used for each displayed claim.

Party registration
When available, Nebraska VoterCheck confirms a candidate’s voter-registration affiliation.
Primary participation
A named partisan primary in the Gage County Electionware report confirms participation in that party’s primary contest. It does not confirm voter registration.
Current filings
The county filing list identifies regular filings, petition candidacies, and declared write-ins; a write-in declaration does not mean the name is printed on the ballot. State and federal races use the final statewide general-election candidate list.
Election results
Gage County vote totals come from its Electionware report, which is labeled UNOFFICIAL RESULTS and covers Gage County reporting only. The official state canvass establishes statewide and multi-county primary nominees.
Election stage
Current listings and primary history are separate. Primary history includes non-advancing candidates, withdrawals, and primary nominees absent from the final general-election list.
Pending verification
When no affiliation source is confirmed, the directory says “Affiliation not yet verified.” No affiliation is inferred.

Important context

What “nonpartisan” means here

A nonpartisan race is one in which party labels do not appear beside candidate names on the ballot. A candidate may still have a publicly documented political affiliation.

Registered affiliation, ballot party, and participation in a party primary are different facts. Each affiliation label identifies which evidence supports it.

County, state, and federal sections also include partisan races. Consult official election materials for the contests on your ballot.

Frequently asked questions

Questions about this reference

Are all races in this directory nonpartisan?

No. In a nonpartisan race, party labels do not appear beside candidate names on the ballot. This directory also includes partisan county, state, and federal races.

Does a listed affiliation mean the candidate is officially running as a party nominee?

Not necessarily. The directory includes races with different ballot rules. A publicly documented political affiliation should not be treated as proof of nomination status; consult official election materials for the specific race.

How was this information verified?

Registered affiliation, ballot party, primary participation, and election results are supported by different sources.

Why are some candidates listed under primary history?

These candidates participated in the primary and are not currently listed for the general election. The card identifies non-advancement, withdrawal, or absence from the final list when supported by a source.

What happens if an affiliation changes?

The entry should be updated when a reliable public source documents the change. Each card shows when its source was reviewed.

Does this site endorse candidates?

Inclusion in this directory does not indicate a Gage County GOP endorsement. Candidates receive the same sourcing and presentation standards.

How can an error be corrected?

Send a supporting public source to the correction contact shown in this guide.
