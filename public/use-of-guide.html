Use of this guide

Updated October 11, 2026

The Gage County GOP publishes Party Reveal as a reference for selected races in Gage County. The guide does not include every contest on your ballot, and inclusion does not indicate an endorsement.

Understanding the evidence

Candidate entries cite public sources and distinguish voter-registration affiliation, party labels in official candidate lists, and participation in a party primary. Each type of evidence establishes different facts.

An unconfirmed affiliation means reviewers have not confirmed a supporting source, and no party is inferred. The directory separates current general-election candidates from primary history.

A declared write-in candidacy does not establish that the candidate’s name is printed on the ballot.

Checking election information

Party Reveal is not an official election authority. Consult official election materials for the contests on your ballot, printed candidate names, polling information, deadlines, and final candidate lists.
