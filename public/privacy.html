Privacy

Updated October 11, 2026

Party Reveal uses Google Analytics 4 on its production website to understand how visitors use the guide. Standard Google Analytics uses cookies and collects usage information.

Information recorded

The site records general actions, such as opening a race, viewing sources, selecting the election calendar, and using search or filters. Custom events do not include search text, candidate names, party choices, or source URLs. Search events report whether results were found.

Page-view settings omit query text and directory fragments. The site disables Google signals and advertising personalization signals. Google processes analytics information under its own privacy policy.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics cookies
  • Google Analytics opt-out add-on for supported browsers

Website hosting

GitHub Pages hosts the website. GitHub describes its processing of visitor information in its privacy statement.
